## §2 Checked and holding
- **Order and the merged warp.**
  - The order is exact: scene → mirror → feedback → bloom → lens → finish (`K/engine/engine.js:65-67`).
  - Merging mirror and feedback into one pass is exact. The mirror is a pure gather (`K/lib/fx.js:75-129`), and the donor's feedback reads the mirror's output at the same pixel (`fx.js:157-171`).
- **No feedback runaway.**
  - The history is written before the bloom, lens and finish, so there is no bloom → history loop.
  - hist × persist (≤ .975, `engine.js:177`) × tint (≤ 1 while PAL ≤ 1) × edge (≤ 1) decays geometrically, and `max()` caps it at the input's running peak. C10 covers the tint guard.
- **No strobe through the feedback.**
  - `max()` with a ≤ .975 decay only slows falls.
  - The lens re-seats fire on onKick: the glitch seed, the drift click, the melt rips (`engine.js:210-222`). onKick is the rise of the gated hit (`host.ts:45`), and `flashGate` never raises its output between allowed onsets (`post.ts:50-62`). So there are ≤ 3 re-seats per s (2 in donor mode).
- **Less raw-kick motion.** The kick push on the feedback zoom moves from a raw kick binding (`KICK_ZOOM`, `builtin.ts:1837`) to the gated, slewed `hot` (F1). That is an I-FL1 gain.
- **Most presets are dimmer.** 106/173 presets have heat < .5, and the median preset's kick glow is ×.73 of the bloomMul-1 baseline (`g1b.txt`). The risk sits in the 11 hot presets and the user's knobs (C1).
- **Analysis off needs no new code.**
  - `gain()` replaces every source with IDLE: levels at .5, `syn_*Level` included (`bind.ts:63-65, :95-99`).
  - `vs3Live.s` is null unless the analyser is `'vs3'` (`app.ts:540`).
  - `boundStep(off)` returns 0 for ⚡ (`post.ts:73`). Kick terms rest; level terms stay constant.
  - The glitch lens still re-rolls on its 3/s clock (`engine.js:213`). That is motion, not light.
- **Toy limiter (F5).** Unchanged: only toys and `native/video` get one (`live.ts:294, :308`). Caveat: a vs3 limitless filter placed over a limited toy re-amplifies it; the C1 limiter covers that.
- **Transitions.** Each scene draws into its own RGBA8 shot (`engine.ts:116, :235-240`), and the 13 transitions mix two finished pictures. The outgoing scene keeps its history. Exception: same-scene fades (C5).
- **The 1.8 s glide.**
  - It is safe for the float light knobs. At 60 fps the cosine's steepest frame moves brightness by ≤ .015 and heat's glow factor by ≤ ×1.04 per frame (`presets.ts:34-36`).
  - `mirror` and `lensStyle` switch at the midpoint (`presets.ts:54`). The fold fade then smooths the mirror over .5 s (`post-shaders.js:157, :185-197`).
  - The history keeps a fading ghost of the old fold. That is visual only.
- **Memory.**
  - The design's +124 MB is right for the history pair at 7.7 MP (16 B/px).
  - Per vs3 layer with today's targets: three rt half-float + depth 12 B/px (`three/host.ts:73`), bloom ≈ 10.7 B/px (`bloom.ts:51-52`), plus the scratch. Total ≈ 47 B/px: ≈ 240 MB at 5.09 MP, ≈ 480 MB for two layers in a fade.
  - That fits in 16 GB. A context loss reloads the page anyway (`engine.ts:18`). 60-full on the 6K display would need ≈ 1.9 GB in a fade; that mode is not realistic there.
- **Lifecycle.**
  - The chain's targets live and die with the native (`host.ts:246-253`). A load that resolves after dispose frees itself (`host.ts:274`).
  - `prepare()` frees every Live except the current and the next between fades (`live.ts:856-863`), so at most 2 chains exist.
  - The new passes sit inside the existing `glState`/`restore` window (`host.ts:236-244`).
- **No name collisions.** None of the 18 look keys collides with the 37 scene-row names in `vs3/params.json` (checked with node).
- **Lens cost in factory presets.** Anamorphic and pixels appear in 0/173 presets. The 27-tap worst case comes only from a user's pick.
- **The 'water' lens style (2 presets).** It is not a style in the original either: it plays as glass ribs (`O/main.js:2349, :15886`, `?? 0`). The generator must map it to 0; step 7's range test catches a −1.
- **MIDI `'*'` is needed.** `midi.ts:76` maps one target per control, and `:82` requires the stage scene. So 4 knobs serve one scene today, as design R1 states. C7 covers the cross-writes.
- **`decay`.** It is the analyser's release, not a feedback term (`post-shaders.js:25-29`). The designer's spec correction holds.
- **Per-frame allocations.** The few small uniform objects that `frame()` returns are not a budget risk. `host.ts:243` already allocates its finish uniforms every frame.

## §3 Verdict
**BLOCKED.** The open HIGHs are C1, C2 and C3. Each one can be answered inside the design; none needs a variant change.

- **Variant A: BLOCKED. Safe to plan after these fixes:**
  1. One scalar light limiter in `frame()`, on real dt, in both directions, over bloom strength, `uGain`, `uVig` and lens amount. It closes C1, C2 and the light part of C6.
  2. H2 in steps 3, 5 and 7, plus the stress row (heat 1, react 1, brightness 2.4, lens 1).
  3. A washout check in step 3 against the thumbs, with a GPU-only AE servo as a contingency step (C3).
  - The rest go in as plan items:
    - C4: the R5 judging point
    - C5: the same-scene fade guard (the bug predates this epic)
    - C6: real dt
    - C7: the `'*'` ref check
    - C8: write the chain off in legacy user copies
    - C9: put the per-scene macro choice on record at the step-3 gate
    - C10: warp guards
    - C11: scratch aliasing
    - C12: async compile
- **Variant B: not recommended.**
  - It shares the chain, so C1–C3 and C10–C12 apply to it too.
  - It adds RGBA16F shots for every scene (`engine.ts:116-118`), a second flash gate, and text and camera layers under the chain.
  - Its per-shot state does avoid C5.
- **Variant C: a valid pick only if the lead or the user decides the macros must follow across scenes.**
  - It shares the chain, so C1–C3, C5, C6 and C10–C12 apply.
  - It removes C7 (a `LookTarget` kind) and C9 (persistent macros), at about twice A's size.

### Checked and holding (recheck)
- **`r` when `stockPrev` = 0.** Only a filter (limitless) in silence has a stock of 0. When sound returns, `r` = 1 lets the glow take the stock scene's own first step, which is today's step; nothing worse.
- **A frame drop with `dtl` capped at 1/60.** A 100 ms frame gets one 60 fps allowance: the light lags but never jumps, and `hot` keeps 34 % per frame (today 57 % at 30 fps).
- **The look arrives.** The level follows at ×4/s. ×16 settles in 2 s; heat 0 → 1 (×256) in 4 s. The extreme case (heat 0 → 1, bloomMul .8 → 2.5 and brightness 1.36 → 2.4: ×4 400) takes ≈ 6 s; after a 1.8 s glide, the lag is about 1.8 s less.
  - Kicks during the catch-up ratchet the level up faster: the stock's rises are taken, and its falls are clipped while the target sits above.
  - What never arrives is the donor's ×14 kick pump. That is `DYN` 1, by the lead's call.
  - A light knob bound to audio is compressed to its slow average.
- **The limiter's bounds.**
  - `uGain` and the glow's follow combined move ≤ ×1.0234 per frame (.0064 luma), because `glow` carries `uGain`.
  - Where the glow sits at or above the stock, its kick step is the stock's in linear light. The concave curve then keeps each pixel's step ≤ the stock's.
  - At stock values the rule is exact, except N6.
- **The servo's own motion.** It is ≤ ×1.0155 per frame at a 10× error (.0043 luma), and the rate cap binds only beyond a 32× error. It reads the picture, not the audio, so analysis-off needs nothing. N2, N3 and N4 cover its edges.
- **C5's guard placement.** All of these reach `begin()`: set-list cues, ← / →, the autopilot and `jump()`. A mid-fade cue back to the outgoing scene still fades, between two different scenes.
