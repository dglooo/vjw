# Research — Rave Lasers (RL-S1: real rigs, figures, realism, sync, rendering, reuse)

## TL;DR
- Real rave rigs are many scanned RGB beam projectors: a club has a few to ~10 plus multi-eye bars, a festival main stage has 30–61 (a 360° record claims 1,100). They sit on upstage truss rows, side or delay towers, downstage diagonals and around the DJ booth. Each projector draws a fan of discrete beams: it holds on points and blanks between them.
- The named figures are fan, sheet / liquid sky, tunnel / cone, crossing grid / cage, chase, grating burst and audience scan. Every one exists only through haze: haze reads smooth, fog reads swirling.
- Sourced realism cues: brightness per beam falls as a fan widens into a sheet (the energy is shared); forward scatter makes beams aimed at the viewer brighter; RGB mixes to white, with colour halos when the beam profiles differ; a short camera exposure catches partial scans (dots, dashes, flicker); an anchor makes a hot dot at a line end.
- Sync: operators tap the tempo, use AutoBPM or DJ timecode. BEYOND effect durations are set in beats or seconds. Vendor presets: chase strobe bursts 6–8 Hz, fans ≤ 4 Hz. Scanners are rated 30 kpps at 8°, and a shape redrawn > 20 times/s reads as static.
- Rendering numbers: our own ribbon quads (Isobars) bill 1.57 / 1.73 ms p95 without haze, and the mip-chain haze adds ≈ 1.3 ms (3.76 ms p95 in a slow-GPU session); the rejected per-pixel fan billed 2.9–3.9 ms. Published analytic methods (a capsule inverse-square integral, the Sun 2005 airlight) avoid raymarching. UE4 shadowed volumetric lasers: ≈ 7 ms for 8 beams on a GTX 1060.

## §1 Real rigs
**Fixture types**
- **Scanned beam / graphics projector (galvo, ILDA / FB4).** Two mirror galvos steer one beam over 40–60°; the speed is rated in pps / kpps (example 30 kpps). Source: [Kvant basics](https://www.kvantlasers.sk/pages/laser-display-technology-basics). Beam examples: Kvant Atom 42 = 7×7 mm, 1 mrad; Atom 12 = 5 mm, < 1 mrad ([Atom 42 sheet](https://www.procom-me.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Atom-42-Specification-Sheet.pdf), [Atom 12](https://www.proxdirect.com/products/view/KVANT-ATOM-12-ATOM-12)).
- **Stepper "beam" units vs galvo animation units.** Stepper motors = simple beams and chases; galvo / ILDA = text, logos, animation ([Starshine, vendor](https://www.starshinelights.com/blogs/news/laser-light-dj-guide)).
- **"Fat beam"** is a visibility choice (a thicker beam for weak haze or high ceilings), not a separate technology ([Starshine, vendor](https://www.starshinelights.com/blogs/news/laser-light-bar-club-dj-stage-guide)). Kvant: a tighter beam reads better until the viewing distance exceeds the eye's resolution; past that, thicker reads better ([Kvant basics](https://www.kvantlasers.sk/pages/laser-display-technology-basics)).
- **Multi-eye bars.** Laserworld BeamBar = a fan of 10 beams, extendable into a "barrier" or chases ([Laserworld](https://www.laserworld.com/en/laser-systems/laserworld/beambar-series.html)); 6-eye RGB bars chase in pairs ([Starshine](https://www.starshinelights.com/blogs/news/rgb-laser-bar-fan-tunnel)).
- **DMX beam / grating fixtures.** ER Beamburst: 6-channel DMX, a single beam or rotating diffraction-grating effects ([ER](https://er-productions.com/items-3/beamburst)). ER BB4: 4 mm beam, 45° X/Y scan, a gobo wheel for line / burst / lumiere / lenticular gratings ([ER BB4](https://www.er-productions.com/items-3/bb4)).

## §2 Figures (what each looks like on camera)
Basis: a scanned projector makes all beam figures, "fans, beam sequences, sheets of light, cones and tunnels" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_lighting_display); [Pangolin beam shows](https://pangolin.com/pages/beam-shows)). ILDA: "the 2D screen doesn't do justice to 3D beams that surround the crowd and the camera" ([ILDA YouTube page](https://ilda.com/youtube.htm), via search ⚠️). Fans, tunnels and sheets look alike in photos and differ in person ([Pangolin types](https://pangolin.com/blogs/news/types-of-laser-shows), via search).

## §3 Realism cues (what makes a beam read as a real laser in footage)
1. **No haze, no beam.** A beam is visible only where it hits particles; clean indoor air shows nothing ([ILDA haze](https://ilda.com/hazefoglasers.htm); [Kvant basics](https://www.kvantlasers.sk/pages/laser-display-technology-basics)). Too much haze ruins the effect (Kvant). Vendor target: a light, even haze, the opposite wall still visible, nudged up before a drop ([Starshine](https://www.starshinelights.com/blogs/news/rgb-laser-bar-fan-tunnel)).
2. **Haze vs fog = smooth vs turbulent.** Haze spreads evenly, so cones and planes look smooth. Fog billows and varies in density, so cones and planes show swirling areas, because the thin beam lights only a cross-section of the fog ([ILDA haze](https://ilda.com/hazefoglasers.htm)). A static beam is hard to tell from a non-volumetric line; motion sells the volume ([UE4 thread, via search](https://realtimevfx.com/t/ue4-volumetric-laser-beams/11363)).
3. **Energy is shared.** "A beam concentrated into a small area will always appear brighter than the same beam spread across a large graphic or sheet"; faster scanners only reduce flicker, they add no brightness ([Liberation forum](https://forum.liberationlaser.com/t/do-you-need-to-adjust-scanner-settings-probably-not/2412)). A higher sample rate dims each sample ([Pangolin wiki](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:vectors_and_points)). So a static single beam reads hotter than a beam inside a wide fan or a sheet.
4. **Hot dots at anchors and the origin.** Extra points at line ends make a visible bright spot ("more energy lands there"). A sample rate too low for the projector shows dots at each point ([Pangolin wiki](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:vectors_and_points)). The aperture glow at the origin: ⚠️ unverified as a sourced statement (no page found); it follows from cue 5 when the beam points at the camera.
5. **Forward scatter = beams aimed at the viewer are brighter.** Haze-size particles scatter strongly forward; the forward lobe is orders of magnitude above the backscatter and grows with size ([arXiv 1211.0878](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0878)). A UE4 laser asset dims the beam with a "fresnel-like" depth fade, brighter when seen head-on along the plane ([UE4 thread](https://realtimevfx.com/t/ue4-volumetric-laser-beams/11363)). Photographers shoot "where the beams point toward the camera" ([laser photo tips, via search](https://www.matthughesphoto.com/h-photography/how-to-photograph-a-laser-light-show.html) ⚠️).
6. **Saturated colour, mixing to white.** R + G + B mix to the secondaries and to white; if the beam size and profile differ between colours, the mixed colours show halos ([Kvant basics](https://www.kvantlasers.sk/pages/laser-display-technology-basics)). Photographers fight clipped white blobs with a smaller aperture to keep the colour saturated ([fireworks analogue, via search](https://www.the-digital-picture.com/Photography-Tips/fireworks.aspx) ⚠️). So a "white-hot core, coloured skirt" is how a camera records an over-exposed coloured beam (⚠️ inference from these two sources).
7. **Scan flicker and dashes on camera.** A fast exposure "may pick up only part of the laser image's refresh" (at 8 refreshes/s, a 1/16 s exposure shows half the graphic); partly scanned areas look dimmer ([Pangolin photo tips](https://pangolin.com/blogs/news/how-to-take-good-laser-photos)). A rolling-shutter sensor exposes row by row, so a fast mirror beam is caught at different positions down the frame and cannot be removed by shutter or frame-rate changes ([Hackaday](https://hackaday.com/2020/03/05/camera-and-code-team-up-to-make-impossible-hovering-laser-effect); [photo forum](https://www.thephotoforum.com/threads/cant-prevent-rolling-shutter-when-recording-lasers.359984/)).
8. **Discrete beams, crisp edges.** Blanking between strokes keeps the beams of a fan separate ([Pangolin wiki](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:vectors_and_points)). Beam divergence is about 1 mrad (Atom 42 sheet, §1), so a beam stays almost constant in width across a stage.
9. **Beams leave the frame.** The 3D beams surround the crowd and the camera ([ILDA YouTube page](https://ilda.com/youtube.htm) ⚠️ via search); a beam end inside the frame happens only where it hits a surface (screen effects need a surface, [Laserworld glossary](https://www.laserworld.com/en/58-terms-and-definitions.html)). ⚠️ "beam ends out of frame" as a camera rule is an inference, not a quote.
10. **Darkness around the beams.** Breakdowns go dark so the bursts land harder ([Ticket Fairy](https://www.ticketfairy.com/blog/visual-language-for-bass-music-festivals-lasers-low-light-and-safety.md)).

## §4 Show sync (how operators lock lasers to music) and typical rates
- **Tempo sources in BEYOND / QuickShow:** tap the BPM label or the space bar (taps averaged), Backspace re-syncs the phase, type a BPM ([Pangolin wiki](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:music_and_beats_overview)); AutoBPM from an audio input, "works best with music that has a strong, clear beat"; a Virtual DJ plugin reads BPM and track position over timecode ([Pangolin sync](https://pangolin.com/blogs/news/synchronize-laser-light-shows-to-music)); MIDI clock (an option to ignore it exists; "Hard Beat Sync" ties the cue player to the beat manager) ([Pangolin wiki, via search](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:music_and_beats_overview)).
- **What the beat drives:** beat-based animations, beat-based effects and QuickTargets beam sequences ([Pangolin wiki](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:music_and_beats_overview)). An oscillating effect's duration is set in seconds (clock icon) or in beats (metronome icon); touching the speed slider drops the effect out of sync until reset ([Pangolin forum / wiki, via search](https://forums.pangolin.com/threads/bpm-beatsync-and-cues-timelines.30092/latest)). Timeline cue lengths do not stretch with BPM; only beat-based effects inside a cue follow it ([same thread](https://forums.pangolin.com/threads/bpm-beatsync-and-cues-timelines.30092/latest); [QuickShow timeline BPM](https://wiki.pangolin.com/quickshow:timeline_bpm)).
- **Live control:** BEYOND has four Live Control groups (Cue, Master, Projection Zone, ProTracks), each with an animation-speed control ([Laserworld BEYOND ch. 4](https://www.laserworld.com/en/product-related-faq/pangolin-beyond/2044-learning-pangolin-beyond-universe-chapter-4-controls-tools.html)); the Virtual Laser Jockey fires cues and effects on the BPM beat or on detected audio beats ([Pangolin VLJ](https://wiki.pangolin.com/beyond:virtual_laser_jockey)); input from PC / touchscreen, MIDI, DMX or a lighting console ([Pangolin live](https://pangolin.com/blogs/news/how-to-make-live-laser-shows)). DJ-side: SoundSwitch, Serato, ShowKontrol over Art-Net or timecode ([DJ TechTools](https://forum.djtechtools.com/t/tutorial-how-to-control-and-sync-lasers-to-your-dj-software-or-setup/78982)).
- **Festival practice:** EDC LV 2014 = one Lasergraph DSP per laser, a grandMA2 for live control, a 60-min timecode show built around nine DJ intros ([Laserworld](https://www.laserworld.com/en/newslist/106-laserworld-news-en/1872-edc-las-vegas-30-laser-systems.html)). Kvant install: Pangolin BEYOND driven from a grandMA3 ([Kvant catalogue](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0311/3415/4891/files/Kvant_Product_Catalogue_2023_web.pdf) ⚠️ via search).

## §5 On-screen rendering techniques and their cost
Frame: 1728×1117 = 1,930,176 px; budget 5 ms GPU per scene (spec R5). Costs below: measured in-house, published, or computed (marked).
| # | Technique | How it works (described, no code) | Cost notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | **Per-pixel additive line glow** (distance to segment) | Every pixel measures its distance to every beam segment; brightness = a falloff of that distance. | b | **Geometry quads (ribbons)** | Native GL lines are 1 px; ANGLE caps `lineWidth` at 1.0, so engines expand each segment into a screen-aligned quad ([Cesium](https://cesium.com/blog/2013/04/22/robust-polyline-rendering-with-webgl/); [DesLauriers "Drawing Lines is Hard"](https://mattdesl.svbtle.com/drawing-lines-is-hard), via search); joins are the hard part ([Kitware](https://www.kitware.com/drawing-lines-in-geojs/)) | Cost ∝ covered pixels, not px × beams.
