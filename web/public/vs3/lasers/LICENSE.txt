plants.bin: four plants from "Anthurium Botany 01" by Poly Haven,
https://polyhaven.com/a/anthurium_botany_01, released under CC0 1.0
(public domain). Downloaded 2026-10-15 from dl.polyhaven.org (glTF
geometry only, no textures). Reduced to positions and indices, scaled
to 1.6 m tall, simplified by 3 cm vertex clustering and quantized to
16 bits. Variants kept: anthurium_botany_01_a, 01_b, 04_d, 06_f.

Format: 'LPLT', u32 plant count, then per plant: f32 half extents
x y z, u32 vertex count, u32 index count, i16 xyz per vertex (padded to
4 bytes), u16 indices (padded to 4 bytes).
