Built-in mesh models for the limitless 3d scene

skull.lm3d, bust.lm3d and hand.lm3d are simplified public domain
models, packed for App by tools/build_limitless_models.mjs.
They are welded, cleaned of loose specks, turned upright, centered,
simplified to 60,000 to 90,000 triangles and stored without textures.
Source files are not in this repository. Download the files below,
rename them to the builder filenames, then run:

  node tools/build_limitless_models.mjs <source folder>
